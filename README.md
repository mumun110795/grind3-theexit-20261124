## About this entry

This entry leans on the most respectable excuse in the resignation
playbook — leaving to spend more time with family — and then quietly
admits the family were strangers until a fire drill introduced them.
The opening stays stiff and sendable, with the formal "I hereby resign"
and "effective immediately" doing the straight-man work, so the small,
warm confession at the end can land as the whole joke in one breath.
